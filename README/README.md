**Complaint Analysis Dashboard for Smart City**



**Statement of Problem**



The modern city has a huge volume of complaints from citizens about garbage disposal, water supply problems, damaged roads, street light defects, traffic jams, drainage systems, among others. It is becoming increasingly challenging to monitor and address such complaints manually as cities evolve. The objective of this project is to analyze these complaints and create a Smart City Complaint Analytics Dashboard.



**Description of the Dataset**

Smart City Complaint Dataset comprises data related to the complaints and their solutions that were analyzed.

**Key Findings**



Garbage Overflow was established as the highest complaint type, with 16 complaint counts recorded.

Complaint trend analysis enabled the identification of recurring civic issues.

Efficiency differed from department to department.

Priority complaints were resolved within a shorter time frame than others.

Insights gained from the dashboard could be used to enhance city performance.



**Recommendations**



Waste management services should be improved to lower Garbage Overflow complaint numbers.

Higher priority complaints should be given more attention when resolving.

Department performance is to be enhanced to ensure prompt responses.

Analysis should be carried out to identify the prevailing issues.

Dashboard information could be used to make relevant decisions.
